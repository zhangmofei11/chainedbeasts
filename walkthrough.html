STORY WALKTHROUGH

Complete chapter-by-chapter guide

📜 CHAPTER 1: AWAKENING

Level 1-1: The Cage

Tutorial level. Learn basic movement and attacks.

  • • Follow the glowing path
  • • Defeat 5 chained wolves to break your first chain
  • • Secret: Hidden area behind the second cage

Level 1-2: First Hunt

Learn combat basics against wild beasts.

  • • Practice dodging enemy attacks
  • • Collect 50 chain fragments
  • • Unlock: First evolution ability

📜 CHAPTER 2: THE PACK

Level 2-1: Wolf Territory

Encounter the Shadow Fenrir pack.

  • • Multiple wolves attack in groups
  • • Use area attacks to handle crowds
  • • Boss: Alpha Wolf - dodge its charge attack

Level 2-3: Bear Cave

Face your first major beast encounter.

  • • Iron Bear has high defense - use combos
  • • Collect 100 fragments for evolution
  • • Boss: Cave Bear - attack during recovery frames

📜 CHAPTER 3: THE TRIAL

Level 3-2: Trial Grounds

Prove your worth as a primal beast.

  • • Survive 3 waves of increasingly strong enemies
  • • Heal pads appear between waves
  • • Boss: Inferno Lion - The ultimate test

⚔️ BOSS STRATEGIES

🦁 Inferno Lion

  • • Avoid Flame Swipe - long range
  • • Wait for Roar cooldown to attack
  • • Ultimate: Dodge to the sides

🐺 Alpha Wolf

  • • Charge attack has tell-tale crouch
  • • Pack summons - clear adds fast
  • • Puncture wound DoT - keep moving
